Chapter 1
Getting Started — Login & Registration
How to create your company account and log in to ZeroPoint ERP for the first time.

1.1 Logging In

Login screen — enter your registered email address and password, then click Login
1
Open the application URL
Navigate to the ZeroPoint ERP URL in your web browser. The Login screen appears by default.
2
Enter your email and password
Type your registered email address and password in the fields shown.
3
Click Login
You are taken to the Dashboard. Your session remains active until you click Logout in the top-right of the header.

1.2 Registering a New Company

Registration form — fill in Company Name, your Full Name, Email, and Password to create your account
1
Click "Register" on the login screen
The registration form appears.
2
Fill in all fields
Company Name — your legal business name
Full Name — your name as the primary administrator
Email — used for login
Password — minimum 8 characters
3
Click Register
The system creates your company, seeds a complete chart of accounts, and logs you in automatically.

Chapter 3
Initial Setup
Complete these steps once before entering any transactions. Proper setup ensures correct GL posting, tax calculations, and document numbering.

3.1 Company Settings

Go to Settings → Company Settings.

Company Settings — enter your company name, address, logo, currency symbol, and document number prefixes
1
Enter company details
Company Name, Address, Phone, Email, Tax Registration Number, and Currency Symbol.
2
Set document prefixes
Configure number prefixes for each document type (e.g. INV-, PO-, BILL-) and the starting sequence number.
3
Save Settings
Changes take effect immediately on new documents.

3.2 Chart of Accounts

Go to Accounting → Chart of Accounts. A full standard chart of accounts is seeded automatically on registration.

Chart of Accounts — standard double-entry account tree with account number, name, type, and current balance. Use the Opening Balance and Year-End Close buttons in the toolbar.
1
Review seeded accounts
Assets (1000–1999), Liabilities (2000–2999), Equity (3000–3999), Revenue (4000–4999), Expenses (5000–5999).
2
Add custom accounts if needed
Click + New Account. Enter Account Number, Name, Type, and Sub-Type. Click Save.

3.3 Opening Balances

If migrating from another system, click the Opening Balance button in the Chart of Accounts toolbar to enter your trial balance as of the migration date.

Post Before Any Transactions
Enter opening balances before creating any invoices or bills. The entry auto-balances via account 3050 Opening Balance Equity.

3.4 Tax Configuration

Go to Settings → Tax. Define all tax rates your business charges on sales and pays on purchases. These rates appear in every invoice, bill, and expense form.

1
Open Tax Settings
Navigate to Settings → Tax. The panel shows all existing tax rates with their name, rate %, active status, and default flag.
2
Click "+ Add Tax"
The Add Tax form slides open. Fill in:
  • Tax Name — e.g. "Standard VAT", "Zero Rate", "Reduced Rate"
  • Rate (%) — numeric percentage, e.g. 17 for 17%
  • Default — tick to pre-select this rate on new documents
3
Save
The rate appears instantly in all document line-item tax dropdowns. You can add multiple rates — only one can be marked as default.
Tax list — existing rates with active toggle and default badge
Add Tax form — enter name and rate percentage
Input Tax Recovery
When a tax rate is applied to a bill or expense, the system automatically splits the tax into account 1250 Input Tax Recoverable — the amount you can reclaim on your VAT return. The Tax Summary report shows the full Output vs. Input tax calculation.

3.5 Shipping Methods (Ship Via)

Go to Settings → Ship Via. Define the carriers and delivery methods your company uses. These appear in the Ship Via dropdown on Sales Orders, Invoices, and Purchase Orders.

1
Click "+ Add Ship Via"
Enter the carrier or method name — e.g. DHL Express, Own Truck, TCS Courier, Walk-in Pickup.
2
Save and activate
The method is active by default and immediately available on all sales and purchase documents. Use the toggle on each row to deactivate without deleting.
Ship Via Configuration — carriers and delivery methods with active/inactive toggle. Active methods appear in the Ship Via dropdown on every sales and purchase document.

Create GRN when goods arrive

Chapter 8
Accounting
Manual journal entries, General Ledger, and Trial Balance for full double-entry bookkeeping control.

9.1 Journal Entries

Go to Accounting → Journal Entries. Use for adjustments, accruals, and corrections not covered by automated documents.

Journal Entries list — all manual journals with entry number, date, description, and posted/draft status
Journal Entry form — debit and credit lines with account selector, amounts, and live balance validation (Debits must equal Credits to post)
1
Click "+ New Journal Entry"
Enter Entry Date, Reference Number, and Description.
2
Add debit and credit lines
Select GL account, enter Debit or Credit amount and description per line. Total Debits must equal Total Credits.
3
Post
Click Post. Posted entries affect the GL immediately. To correct: click Reverse to create an equal opposite entry, then post the corrected one.

9.2 General Ledger

Go to Accounting → General Ledger. Shows every transaction posted to a specific account with running balances.

General Ledger — select an account and date range to see opening balance, all transactions (date, entry no, description, debit, credit), and closing balance

9.3 Trial Balance

Go to Accounting → Trial Balance. Lists all GL accounts with their balance. Total Debits must equal Total Credits.

Trial Balance — all accounts listed with debit or credit balance as of the selected date. Grand total row shows Debits = Credits when balanced.

9.2 Approval Workflows

Go to Settings → Approval Workflows. Configure rules that automatically route documents to a designated approver based on document type and amount threshold — preventing unauthorised spending without any manual process.

Setting Up a Rule

1
Click "+ Add Rule"
The rule form opens on the right side of the panel.
2
Configure the rule fields
FieldDescription
Document TypePurchase Order, Bill, Invoice, or Expense
Min AmountRule applies when document total is ≥ this value (0 = always applies)
Max AmountUpper limit — leave blank for no upper limit
Approver RoleThe role whose members must approve (e.g. CFO, Manager)
Step OrderFor multi-step approval chains — lower numbers run first
3
Save the rule
The rule activates immediately. Any document matching the criteria will automatically move to Pending Approval when submitted.
Rules list — all active rules showing document type, amount range, and approver role
Add Rule form — example: Purchase Orders over $5,000 require CFO approval

Acting on Pending Approvals

Users with the approver role see a red badge on the Approvals button in the header. Click it to open the Pending Approvals inbox.

Approve
Click Approve on any request. The document moves to its next status and is processed normally. The action is logged in the audit trail.
Reject
Click Reject and enter a reason. The document is held and the requester can see the rejection reason. They can revise and resubmit.

9.3 Recurring Documents

Recurring Documents — list of active templates with document type, frequency, next run date, and enable/disable toggle
1
Create a template
Select document type (Invoice / Bill / Expense), set the schedule (Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Quarterly / Annually), Start Date, End Date.
2
Fill in template details
Set customer / vendor, line items, amounts, and tax — exactly as they should appear on each generated document.
3
Activate
The background scheduler generates the document automatically at 06:05 UTC each day when it falls due. No manual action needed.
Chapter 10
Year-End Close
At the end of each financial year, transfer all revenue and expense balances to Retained Earnings and reset P&L accounts to zero for the new year.

Go to Accounting → Chart of Accounts and click the amber Year-End Close button in the toolbar.

Run Only After All Transactions Are Posted
Perform year-end close after all invoices, bills, and expenses for the financial year are fully approved and posted.
Year-End Close panel — fiscal year end date picker, Preview Close button, and history of all previous year-end closes
1
Set Fiscal Year End Date
Enter the last day of your financial year (e.g. 2025-12-31 for calendar year, 2025-06-30 for June year-end).
2
Click "Preview Close"
Every Revenue and Expense account appears with current balance → 0.00 after close, and the net amount flowing to Retained Earnings.
3
Tick confirmation and Post
Read and tick the confirmation checkbox, then click Post Year-End Close. All P&L accounts reset to zero. Retained Earnings increases by the net income.
GL Posting — Year-End Close (Profitable Year)
DR 4XXX All Revenue Accounts [total revenue]
CR 5XXX All Expense Accounts [total expenses]
CR 3100 Retained Earnings [net income]
One Close Per Year
The system blocks duplicate closes for the same fiscal year. To redo: reverse the YEC journal entry, then re-run the close.
